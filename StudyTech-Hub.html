StudyTech Hub

Technology Made Accessible.

StudyTech Hub is a modern and responsive technology service platform designed to provide students and the local community with convenient and accessible computer and technical support services. The platform offers computer access, printing services, document assistance, technical support, and other practical technology solutions for students, individuals, and community members. Built with a clean and user-friendly interface, the website combines essential digital services with an organized browsing experience to make everyday technology needs more convenient, accessible, and reliable.

Technologies Used

HTML CSS JavaScript

Key Features

  • Responsive Layout: Adapts smoothly to desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
  • Computer Services: Provides accessible computer-related services for students and community members.
  • Printing & Documents: Offers convenient printing, document preparation, and related support services.
  • Technical Support: Provides practical technology assistance for common digital and computer-related needs.
